Our Story

Rooted in tradition. Baked with care.

How It All Began

Knead Bakery was founded in August 2006 at Wembley Square in Cape Town, starting as a small family-run artisan bakery café. From day one, our goal was simple: create honest food using traditional methods and locally sourced seasonal ingredients.

Over the years, Knead has grown to five stores across Cape Town — in Constantia, Muizenberg, Panorama, Sanlam, and Newlands. Our bakers and chefs work openly by hand each day, kneading, slow-proofing, and baking throughout the day with care and craftsmanship.

Our Mission

To create simple, honest food using locally sourced seasonal ingredients, baked fresh daily with traditional methods and care.

Our Vision

To remain a beloved Cape Town artisan bakery that brings warmth, comfort, and quality to every customer while maintaining our commitment to craftsmanship and community.

Our Values

  • Craftsmanship — Traditional baking methods, done properly.
  • Quality — Only the best locally sourced ingredients.
  • Community — A warm space for locals and visitors alike.
  • Honesty — Simple food, made fresh, no shortcuts.
