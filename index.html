Welcome to Knead Bakery

Rooted in traditional baking methods, we create simple, honest food using locally sourced seasonal ingredients. Our bakers work openly by hand each day — kneading, slow-proofing, and baking with care and craftsmanship.

What We're Known For

Artisan Bread

Slow-proofed, hand-shaped, and baked fresh throughout the day using traditional methods.

Breakfast & Brunch

Hearty, seasonal dishes served from early morning across all five Cape Town locations.

Pastries & Confections

Croissants, tarts, and sweet treats made with real butter and quality ingredients.
